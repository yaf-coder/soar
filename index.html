Yale College Council SOAR

Help us protect the Yale experience.

SOAR is a student-led giving week for clubs at Yale.

Founded in 1972, the Yale College Council seeks to solve campus issues and connect the students with the administration. We've recently worked on bringing back summer storage, as well as advocating for summer grants!

Student priorities

The Yale Experience is Fading

The YCC is working with the administration to preserve these institutions of the Yale Experience.

Butteries & Dining halls

Butteries have been shut down and dining halls are restricted.

Educational Tools

Summer storage was cut. Printing and course materials costs keep growing. These hit FGLI students hardest.

Mentorship

We want to connect with Yale graduates for advice on academics and life beyond Yale.

Intramurals & Spring Fling

We want to support intramural sports, Spring Fling and other fun student activities on campus.

Student organizations

Campus Involvement

We want to keep making that happen.
